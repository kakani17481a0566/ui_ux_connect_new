## Brand & Style

This design system embodies a warm, accessible, and friendly educational technology aesthetic designed specifically to bridge communication between schools, parents, and children. The visual tone is approachable, optimistic, and reassuring, fostering trust while remaining playful and engaging.

We utilize a **Tactile / Modern** hybrid style combining clean rounded containers with subtle dimensional cues (soft shadows and friendly gradients) to create a welcoming, tactile UI that feels safe and easy to navigate for users of all technical comfort levels.

## Colors

The color palette centers on vibrant cyan and turquoise primary tones inspired by neural connections, supported by deep navy for high-contrast, readable typography. Warm accents like sunny yellow and soft coral provide delightful, child-friendly touchpoints without overwhelming the interface.

## Typography

We use **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all levels for its soft, rounded, and welcoming geometric letterforms. It ensures exceptional legibility on mobile viewports while maintaining an optimistic, approachable tone suitable for families and educators.

## Layout & Spacing

Designed primarily for mobile app experiences using a fluid layout grid. The rhythm relies on 16px outer margins and gutters, with consistent 8px/16px component spacing increments to ensure touch targets remain comfortable and spacious.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is conveyed through soft, ambient shadows with low opacity and generous blur radii, paired with clean rounded surfaces. This creates a friendly, tactile card-based hierarchy that lifts interactive elements gracefully off the background without feeling harsh or overly corporate.

## Shapes

The design system employs a pill-shaped and heavily rounded shape language (`roundedness` level 3). Cards, buttons, and interactive containers feature generous radii (16px to full pill shapes) to evoke a friendly, non-threatening, and child-safe atmosphere.

## Components

### Buttons
Primary buttons feature full pill-shaped rounding (`9999px`), vibrant cyan/turquoise fills, deep navy or white high-contrast text, and a soft drop shadow on hover/press states. Secondary buttons use transparent or tinted backgrounds with matching rounded borders.

### Cards
Feature generous corner radii (16px–24px), clean white or tinted pastel backgrounds, subtle ambient shadows, and internal padding of 16px to 24px. Ideal for grouping daily updates, milestones, and child progress metrics.

### Chips & Badges
Pill-shaped tag elements used for status indicators, subjects, and categories. They combine soft pastel backgrounds with darker text for optimal readability.

### Input Fields
Rounded text inputs with soft neutral borders, clear focus states highlighted in primary cyan, and friendly placeholder text in muted navy tones.

### Lists & Checkboxes
Clean list rows separated by subtle dividers or card groupings. Checkboxes and radio buttons feature rounded, tactile styling with high-contrast checkmarks.
